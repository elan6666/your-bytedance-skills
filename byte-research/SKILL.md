# Byte Research

Research the questions that affect the decision; do not perform a generic market
survey by default.

## Approach

1. Identify the decision and the evidence that could change it.
2. Search current primary or authoritative sources when facts may have changed.
3. Compare relevant alternatives, including substitutes when useful.
4. Separate sourced facts, interpretation, and uncertainty.
5. Translate findings into concrete implications or recommendations.

Choose source depth and breadth according to consequence. Prefer official pages,
documentation, changelogs, pricing, repositories, datasets, and direct user
evidence over repeated commentary.

Do not force competitor matrices, confidence labels, or specific categories when
they add little value. Cite current web claims near the claims they support.

Save research only when it will be reused, audited, or handed off. Use the
project's existing convention or a concise `.byte-os/RESEARCH.md`; do not require
both research and competitor files.

If direct evidence disproves a consequential prior assumption, add a concise
entry to `.byte-os/LESSONS.md` with the correction, supporting evidence, and a
future verification rule. Do not turn changing external facts into personal
mistakes unless the earlier claim lacked appropriate verification.
